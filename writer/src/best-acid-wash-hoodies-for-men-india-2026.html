What the Wash Actually Does to a Hoodie

Acid wash is a finishing step done after the hoodie is dyed and sewn. The garments tumble with pumice stones soaked in a bleaching agent, and wherever a stone presses into the fabric, dye is lifted out. The result is pale, marbled streaks on a dark base. Our acid wash t-shirt guide explains the process and how it differs from stone wash and mineral wash in more detail.

Why hoodies take the wash differently from tees

A hoodie has more going on than a tee: a thick body, a double-layer hood, ribbed cuffs and hem, and heavy seams. Each part takes the wash differently. Seams and ribbing usually come out lighter, because they stand proud and catch more of the stones, while the inside of the hood stays darker. That uneven contrast is a big part of why a washed hoodie looks vintage rather than just faded.

Why 300 GSM matters here

Bleach and abrasion take something out of cotton. On a thin 220 to 250 GSM hoodie, the wash can leave the fabric limp and the cuffs stretched. At 300 GSM there is enough material to absorb that and still hold its shape, which is why MotorBae built the whole acid washed range on 300 GSM, 100% cotton.

Choosing a Colour: Black, Navy, Bottle Green or Maroon

The same wash looks very different on each base colour, so it is worth choosing deliberately.

  • Black washes out to a charcoal grey with white marbling. The most versatile and the easiest to pair; it goes with anything dark or neutral.
  • Navy Blue turns a slate blue with pale streaks, close to washed denim. Works well with black or grey bottoms and white sneakers.
  • Bottle Green becomes a muted olive green. The most outdoorsy of the four and a natural match for khaki or black.
  • Maroon washes to a dusty brick red. The boldest choice; keep the rest of the outfit black or grey.

How an Acid Washed Hoodie Ages

The marbled pattern does not wash out, because it is made by removing dye, not by adding it. What changes over time is the base colour and the hand feel. In the first few months the cotton softens and the hood relaxes. After a full winter of regular washing, the dark areas slowly lighten towards the streaks, so the contrast drops and the hoodie looks more evenly worn. That is the vintage wash look most people are after.

What you do not want is the print cracking or the hem twisting. Both come from thin fabric and hot washing, not from the acid wash itself. A heavyweight hoodie washed cold and inside out avoids both.

Care

MotorBae's care instruction for its acid washed hoodies is simple: wash inside out in cold water, dry on low heat, and turn it inside out before ironing. Add two more habits for the first few washes: wash it with dark clothes only, because a washed garment can still release a little loose dye, and dry it in shade rather than strong sun so the base colour does not go flat.

Does an acid washed hoodie fade over time?

The marbled pattern stays, because it is made by removing dye. The darker base colour slowly lightens with washing, which softens the contrast and gives a more worn-in look. Washing cold and inside out slows it down.

Should I size up in an oversized acid washed hoodie?

Can I wear an acid wash tee with an acid washed hoodie?

Yes, as long as the colours differ. A bottle green or maroon washed tee under a black washed hoodie looks deliberate. Matching the same colour top to bottom tends to look flat.
