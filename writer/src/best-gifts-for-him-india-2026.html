Not Sure Which Scene He Is Into? Look for These Clues

You do not need to ask him directly and spoil the surprise. Most car and bike people leave plenty of hints:

  • His phone wallpaper and lock screen. The car on it is almost always the car he wants. A white Japanese coupe points to JDM; a red Italian car to Ferrari; a big American car to muscle.
  • What he watches on weekends. Formula 1 on Sunday afternoons means the Motorsports Division. Drift and touge videos mean JDM. Drag racing clips mean American muscle.
  • The stickers on his bike or laptop. Team logos, tuner brands and racing numbers are a direct shopping list.
  • The car he owns, or grew up with. A Gypsy or Thar owner will love the Jimny prints; anyone who talks about their family's old Contessa or Ambassador belongs in the Indian Division.
  • His friends. One message to a friend from his car meet crew will tell you more than an hour of scrolling.

If you still are not sure, the safest gifts for him are the ones that suit the whole hobby rather than one car: a heavyweight hoodie, a cap, or a regular fit tee with a broad motorsport theme. When in doubt, go to the {{C:the-t-shirt-store|T-Shirt Store}} and pick by colour he already wears.
