Types of Track Pants for Men

Sweatpants

Heavier cotton or cotton-rich terry or fleece, usually with a relaxed or loose leg. The warmest and most comfortable, and the best choice for winter and travel.

Joggers

Tapered towards the ankle with a cuffed hem. Neater than sweatpants and show off sneakers well.

Sport track pants

Lightweight polyester, quick-drying, often with zipped pockets and side stripes. Built for training, not lounging.

Tracksuits

A matching jacket and track pant. Mens tracksuits are back in fashion as a full look, and a matching hoodie and sweatpants set gives a softer version of the same idea.

Not riding pants. No track pant protects you on a motorcycle. Riders need riding pants with abrasion-resistant fabric and knee armour; our riding gear guide explains what to look for.

Weight matters as much as fibre. A 260 GSM terry like MotorBae's holds its shape at the knee and does not go see-through when it stretches, which is where cheap track pants fail first. A little polyester in the blend helps the fabric keep its shape and colour.

Fit: Loose, Straight or Tapered

Loose and relaxed fits are the current streetwear look and pair with oversized tees and hoodies. Tapered joggers look sportier and neater with fitted tops. Whatever you choose, check the length: the hem should break lightly at the shoe, not pool around the ankle, and an elastic or drawstring waist should sit comfortably without needing to be pulled tight.

Five Quick Quality Checks

  • The waistband: a wide, firm elastic with a real drawstring stays comfortable all day; a thin band rolls over.
  • The knees: stretch the fabric over your knee. If you can see through it, it will bag and thin out fast.
  • The pockets: deep enough for a phone so it does not slide out when you sit in a car.
  • The seams: flat, even stitching on the inner leg, where most cheap pairs split first.
  • The weight: heavier terry or fleece holds its shape far longer than thin jersey. If the GSM is listed, use it to compare.

Sizing Track Pants Online

Sizing is where most online track pant orders go wrong, because waist sizes are rarely printed on elastic-waist bottoms. Measure a pair you already like: lay them flat, measure across the waistband unstretched, and measure the inside leg from crotch to hem. Compare those numbers with the product page rather than relying on S, M or L alone. If you prefer a slouchy fit, go one size up; if you are between sizes in a loose cut like the MB Essentials sweatpants, your usual size will already have room. MotorBae accepts size exchanges within 7 days of delivery on unworn pieces with tags.

Care

Wash track pants and sweatpants inside out in cold water and skip high-heat drying, which shrinks cotton and breaks down elastic waistbands faster than anything else. Terry and fleece pill less if washed with similar fabrics rather than with jeans and towels.

What is the difference between track pants and sweatpants?

Track pants are usually lightweight polyester built for sport. Sweatpants are heavier cotton or terry made for comfort and warmth. Joggers are a tapered version of either.

Does MotorBae sell tracksuits?

No. MotorBae makes MB Essentials sweatpants and hoodies that can be worn together as a matching set, but not a polyester tracksuit.

Can I wear track pants for riding?

No. Track pants offer no abrasion protection. Wear proper riding pants with knee armour on a motorcycle.

What fabric is best for winter track pants?

Heavy cotton-rich terry or fleece. Around 260 GSM terry is warm, soft and holds its shape without feeling bulky.
