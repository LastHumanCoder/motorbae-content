Replica Jersey, Team Tee or Fan Tee: Which One Do You Want?

Replica F1 jersey

A replica of what the team wears in the garage: technical polyester, sponsor logos, team crest, usually a slim athletic cut. An F1 jersey looks the part at a race or watch party, but polyester traps heat in Indian weather and it rarely works as an everyday shirt. If you want an f1 jersey India buyers can get without importing, check team kit makers' local stores first.

Official team tee

A cotton or cotton-blend t-shirt with the team logo, sold under licence. More wearable than a jersey and cheaper, though still priced above a normal tee.

Fan-designed F1 t-shirt

A design that celebrates a car, driver number or moment in the sport without copying team kit. This is the f1 t shirt you can wear to college, the office or a Sunday ride, which is why it is the one most fans wear most often. MotorBae's Motorsports Division sits here.

How to Spot a Fake F1 Jersey or Tee

  • Price check: an official replica jersey is never priced like a basic tee.
  • Licence tags: look for the team or F1 licence on the swing tag and the inner neck label.
  • Sponsor logos: copies often get the shape, spacing or colour slightly wrong, or show last season's sponsors.
  • Seller: buy from the brand's own site or verified marketplace store.

Fan designs are not fakes as long as they do not pretend to be official. For the wider picture, including caps and gifts, see our F1 merchandise India guide.

Care: Keeping Team Colours Bright

Whichever formula 1 t shirt India weather puts through its paces, the care routine is the same. Wash it inside out in cold water so the print never rubs against other clothes, skip bleach, and dry it in the shade, because strong sun fades reds and blues fastest. Never iron over the print; turn the tee inside out first. Polyester jerseys should go on a gentle cycle and never into a hot dryer, which can melt heat-applied sponsor logos.
