What Actually Makes a Hoodie Oversized

A bigger chest number alone does not make an oversized hoodie. Buy a regular hoodie two sizes up and you get the width, but the shoulders slide off, the cuffs cover your hands and the hem hangs like a sack. A properly cut oversized hoodie gets the shape from four details:

1. Dropped shoulders

The shoulder seam sits a few inches down the upper arm instead of on the shoulder bone. This is the single biggest signal. It gives the relaxed, sloping line and lets the hoodie fit over a tee or two without pulling. A drop shoulder hoodie looks oversized even when the chest is not much wider than a regular one.

2. Extended sleeves

Because the shoulder is lower, the sleeves need to be longer so the cuff still lands at the wrist. The slight stacking at the cuff is part of the look.

3. A roomy hood

An oversized hood sits open around the shoulders and has some volume when it is down. A small, tight hood on a big body looks wrong.

4. Heavy fabric

Volume needs weight to drape. At 300 GSM or heavier, the extra fabric falls in clean folds. In thin fleece, the same volume just billows.

How to Size an Oversized Hoodie

Start with your normal size and decide how much drape you want.

  • Relaxed oversized (most men): take your usual size. On MotorBae's acid washed hoodies you get the dropped shoulder and long sleeves with the hem sitting just below the belt.
  • Very baggy: size up one. The hem drops past the hips and the body hangs wider. Works on tall men; can swamp shorter frames.
  • Neater oversized: if you are between sizes, go with the smaller one. The dropped shoulder keeps it relaxed.

The acid washed hoodie chart runs from a 38 inch chest and 25 inch length on XS to 50 inches and 31 inches on 3XL. Measure a hoodie you already like, lay it flat, double the chest width and compare.

Layering an Oversized Hoodie

The oversized cut is made for layering, as long as you keep the proportions right.

  • Under it: a heavy tee whose hem sits shorter than the hoodie's. An oversized tee that hangs out below looks messy; a boxy tee stays hidden. Our boxy fit t-shirt guide explains why.
  • Over it: an oversized hoodie does not fit well under a slim jacket. Under a riding jacket, the regular Camaro Z/28 is the better pick; our jacket guide covers what to wear on top.
  • Bottoms: straight or relaxed trousers, or sweatpants like the {{P:mb-essentials-sweatpants-1|MB Essentials Sweatpants}}. Skinny bottoms under an oversized top make the top look bigger than it is.
  • Riding: a pullover hoodie is a warm mid layer, not protection. See hoodie vs t-shirt for winter riding.

Care

An oversized hoodie loses its shape when the hem and cuffs stretch. Wash inside out in cold water and dry on low heat, which is MotorBae's care instruction for the acid washed range, and fold rather than hang a heavyweight hoodie, because hanging stretches the shoulders over time.

Should I size up for an oversized hoodie?

Usually not. A properly cut oversized hoodie already has dropped shoulders and longer sleeves, so your normal size gives the relaxed look. Size up one only if you want a very baggy fit.

What is the difference between an oversized and a regular fit hoodie?

An oversized hoodie has a dropped shoulder seam, extended sleeves and a roomier hood. A regular fit hoodie has the seam on the shoulder and sits closer to the body. On MotorBae's hoodies the chest measurement is similar at M; the cut is what changes.

Can I wear an oversized hoodie under a jacket?

Under a loose overshirt or puffer, yes. Under a fitted riding jacket, the bulk bunches at the shoulders, so a regular fit hoodie such as MotorBae's Camaro Z/28 works better.

Does MotorBae make zip oversized hoodies?

No. All MotorBae hoodies are pullovers, which are warmer because there is no zip line. For a zip hoodie, H&M and other high-street brands carry plain options.
