Where to Buy Automotive T-Shirts in India

Direct from the brand

The best option for range, sizing information and returns. Brand stores list the fabric weight, show the print on real people, and handle exchanges directly. MotorBae, for example, accepts size exchanges within 7 days of delivery.

Marketplaces

Convenient, but the same print can appear from several sellers with very different quality, and copies of popular designs are common. Check the seller name matches the brand.

Some brands and clubs sell at meets and track days. Great for limited runs; the trade-off is no size exchange once you leave. Our car meet outfit guide covers what to wear there.

Team and manufacturer stores for licensed logos and kits.

What to Check Before Buying Car Enthusiast T-Shirts

  • GSM: under 160 GSM tends to fade and crack quickly. Around 180 GSM is a good regular tee; 240 GSM and above is a heavyweight oversized tee.
  • Specificity: does the print reference a real car, livery or moment, or is it a generic outline? The specific ones are the tees people keep.
  • Fit: regular fit for layering and everyday wear, oversized with dropped shoulders for streetwear.
  • Care label: printed tees last longest washed inside out in cold water.
  • Returns: buy where you can exchange sizes.
  • Photos on people: a print that looks sharp on a flat lay can sit too high or too low on the body. Brands that shoot every tee on real people, next to real cars, make that easy to judge.
