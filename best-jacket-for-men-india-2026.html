Riding Jackets: What Actually Protects You

A biker jacket that looks the part and a riding jacket that protects you are not the same thing. When you shop for a riding jacket for men, check these first:

  • Armour: CE certified armour at the shoulders and elbows as a minimum, ideally with a back protector. CE Level 2 absorbs more impact than Level 1.
  • Abrasion resistance: leather or high-denier textile on the impact zones. Fashion jackets in thin polyester will not survive a slide.
  • Fit: snug enough that the armour stays over your joints when you stretch to the handlebar. A loose riding jacket lets the armour move out of place.
  • Season: mesh for hot months, textile with a removable thermal liner for winter. One liner jacket covers most of India's riding year.
  • Visibility: reflective panels matter more than colour for night riding on Indian highways.

Casual Jackets for Men: The Main Types

Puffer

The warmest per gram and the best choice for North Indian winters and hill trips. Choose a slim or regular puffer for daily wear and a longer one for real cold.

Denim or trucker

The most versatile casual jacket. Works over a tee or hoodie from October to February in most cities.

Bomber

Short, with ribbed cuffs and hem. Pairs well with cargo pants and sneakers; see our cargo pants style guide for combinations.

Windcheater or shell

Light and packable, blocks wind and drizzle. Great over a hoodie, which provides the warmth while the shell blocks the wind.

Zip sweatshirt or zip hoodie

Not a jacket, but in Mumbai, Bengaluru or Chennai a zip sweatshirt is often all the jacket anyone needs. For heavier warmth, a pullover hoodie does the same job better.

Which Jacket for Which City

India's winter is really several different winters, so the right jacket for men in Delhi is overkill in Chennai.

  • Delhi, Chandigarh, Lucknow, Jaipur: real cold from December to early February, especially early morning. A puffer or a lined riding jacket over a heavyweight hoodie.
  • Pune, Hyderabad, Bengaluru: cool mornings and evenings, warm afternoons. A denim jacket, bomber or shell over a tee, or just a hoodie.
  • Mumbai, Chennai, Kolkata: mild most of the season. A zip sweatshirt or light hoodie is usually enough off the bike; riders still need a mesh or textile riding jacket year round.
  • Hill trips and Himalayan rides: layer everything: thermal, hoodie, insulated jacket, shell, gloves and a balaclava.

Fit and Care

Try a riding jacket on over the layers you will actually wear under it. If it only fits over a t-shirt, it will be too tight in December. For casual jackets, the shoulder seam should sit at your shoulder, and the sleeve should end at the wrist bone with your arms relaxed.

Care matters for both. Riding jackets usually need the armour removed before washing, and textile ones should never go in a hot dryer. Hoodies last longest washed inside out in cold water and dried on low heat. For a full cold-weather wardrobe that ties this together, see our winter outfits guide.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which is the best riding jacket for men in India?

Look for CE certified armour at the shoulders and elbows, abrasion-resistant leather or textile, and a fit that keeps the armour in place. Rynox, Viaterra and Royal Enfield are well-established options in India.

Can I wear a hoodie under a riding jacket?

Yes, and it is one of the best ways to stay warm. Choose a regular fit hoodie so it does not bunch under the armour, such as MotorBae's 320 GSM Camaro Z/28 hoodie, and add a balaclava under the helmet.

Does MotorBae sell jackets?

No. MotorBae makes heavyweight hoodies, tees, sweatpants and riding accessories such as balaclavas. Its hoodies are designed to layer under a riding jacket or replace a light jacket off the bike.

Is a biker jacket the same as a riding jacket?

Not always. Many fashion biker jackets have no armour and thin fabric. A riding jacket is built to protect you in a fall, with armour and abrasion-resistant materials.

What is the warmest casual jacket for Indian winters?

A puffer is the warmest for its weight and suits North Indian winters and hill trips. A shell or windcheater over a heavy hoodie is a flexible alternative for milder cities.
