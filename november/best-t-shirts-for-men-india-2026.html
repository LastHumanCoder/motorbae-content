Types of T-Shirts for Men, and When to Wear Each

Regular fit t shirt

Follows the body without clinging, with the shoulder seam on your shoulder. The most versatile t shirt for men: office casual Fridays, under an overshirt, on the bike. MotorBae's Camaro Z/28 Regular Fit and the Jimny G.O.A.T Regular Fit are 180 GSM cotton.

Oversized t shirt

Dropped shoulders, wider body, longer sleeves. Relaxed and current, best with straight or relaxed bottoms. A good oversized t shirt needs heavier fabric, or it just looks like a tee in the wrong size. Our oversized fit t-shirt guide explains how one should sit, and the boxy fit guide covers the shorter, wider cut.

Graphic t shirt

The tee that says something. A graphic t shirt is only as good as its print method and the fabric under it; our printed t-shirts guide explains screen print, DTG and embroidery.

Plain t shirt

The foundation. White, black, grey and navy cover almost every outfit. Buy these on fabric quality, not price.

Terry t-shirt

A heavier knit with small loops on the inside, usually a cotton-polyester blend. It feels soft, holds a structured shape and works well from October to February. The Toyota MR2 Heritage Oversized uses a 260 GSM French terry loop knit.

Fabric: What to Look For on the Label

  • 100 percent cotton: breathes best in Indian heat. Combed cotton is smoother and pills less. A cotton t shirt is the right default for most days.
  • Bio-washed cotton: treated to remove loose fibres, so it feels softer and pills less over time. Several MotorBae regular fit tees use it.
  • Cotton-polyester blends: common in terry and melange fabrics. A small amount of polyester helps the tee keep its shape; too much and it stops breathing.
  • Pre-shrunk fabric: worth looking for so your size stays your size after the first wash.

Fit and Neckline

For a regular fit, the shoulder seam should sit at the edge of your shoulder and the hem should cover your belt. For oversized, the shoulder seam drops down the arm by design; MotorBae recommends ordering one size lower than your regular fit size in its oversized tees. All MotorBae tees are round neck, which suits most face shapes and layers cleanly under a shirt.

Exchanges: MotorBae accepts size exchanges within 7 days of delivery on unworn pieces with tags.

Buying T-Shirts for Men Online: A Quick Checklist

  • GSM listed? If the listing hides the fabric weight, assume it is light.
  • Size chart in inches or centimetres? Measure a tee you already like flat across the chest and compare. Do not rely on S, M and L alone; they vary between brands.
  • Real photos on people? Model shots show how the fit and length actually sit.
  • Exchange policy? A clear size exchange window takes the risk out of trying a new brand.

How Many T-Shirts Does a Man Need?

A practical rotation for most men in India is around ten:

  • Four plain tees in white, black, grey and navy, for layering and smart-casual days.
  • Three graphic tees that reflect what you are into. If that is cars, see our picks of the best car t-shirt brands in India and automotive t-shirts.
  • Two oversized or terry tees for weekends and cooler evenings.
  • One tee for the gym or ride that you do not mind getting sweaty.

For winter, a heavyweight hoodie over a tee covers most days; see the winter outfits guide.

Care: Make Every Tee Last Longer

Wash inside out in cold water, dry in the shade or on low heat, and iron inside out. Hang heavier oversized and terry tees to dry rather than wringing them, which stretches the neck. Fold tees instead of hanging them for storage so the shoulders do not stretch.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which brand makes the best t-shirts for men in India?

It depends on the type. For graphic and car culture tees on proper fabric, MotorBae is our top pick. For plain premium basics, Uniqlo; for affordable plain tees, H&M; for everyday crew necks, Jockey; and for budget prints, Bewakoof.

What GSM is best for a men's t-shirt?

180 GSM is ideal for a regular fit tee you wear every day. For oversized tees with big prints, 240 to 260 GSM holds its shape better. Anything under 160 GSM is too thin for most uses.

Should I buy regular fit or oversized t-shirts?

Regular fit is more versatile and works under shirts and jackets. Oversized is relaxed and current, best with straight or relaxed bottoms. Most men benefit from owning both.

Is 100 percent cotton better than a cotton blend?

For breathability in Indian weather, yes. Blends with a little polyester keep their shape better and are common in terry fabrics. Avoid tees that are mostly polyester for daily wear.

How do I stop t-shirts from shrinking?

Buy pre-shrunk fabric where possible, wash in cold water and avoid hot dryers. Drying in the shade or on low heat keeps both size and colour.
