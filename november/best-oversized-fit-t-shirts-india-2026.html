How an Oversized Fit T-Shirt Should Actually Sit

Four checkpoints tell you whether an oversized tee fits or just looks big.

1. The shoulder seam drops, but not too far

On a drop shoulder tee the seam should sit roughly two to four inches down the upper arm. Any further and the sleeve starts at your bicep and the whole tee looks borrowed.

2. Sleeves end between mid-bicep and elbow

Wider and longer than a regular tee's sleeve, but not past the elbow.

3. The body hangs straight

An oversized fit t shirt should fall straight from the chest to the hem with a few inches of room on each side. If it pulls across the chest or stomach, size up. If the sides fold over themselves, size down.

4. The hem sits at the hip, not the thigh

The hem should land between the belt and the middle of the hip. Past mid-thigh is too long for most men.

Which Size to Order

MotorBae's own guidance on its oversized tees is to order one size lower than your regular fit. That is a good default, but it depends on how much drape you want:

  • Neat oversized: one size lower than your regular. A regular M (40 inch chest) becomes an oversized S at 41 to 42 inches: a drop shoulder and a little more room, without much extra length.
  • Classic oversized: your regular size. A regular M becomes an oversized M at 43 to 44 inches and two inches longer. This is the full drop shoulder drape most streetwear photos show.
  • Very baggy: one size up. Only works if you are tall; on a shorter frame the hem drops to the thigh.

The easiest check: lay your favourite tee flat, measure across the chest just below the armpits, double it, and compare with the chart. Remember the 260 GSM terry tees run an inch shorter than the French Terry ones in the same size.

240 vs 260 GSM: Does the Fabric Change the Fit?

Yes, more than most people expect. An oversized fit t shirt is extra fabric by design, and how that fabric behaves decides whether it looks intentional.

  • Under 200 GSM: an oversized cut in light jersey clings to the stomach and flaps at the sleeves. It looks like a tee that is too big rather than one cut oversized.
  • 240 GSM French Terry: the loop-knit back gives body and a clean drape. The shape holds, but it still moves. This is MotorBae's premium oversized fabric.
  • 260 GSM terry: heavier and a little stiffer, so the tee stands away from the body more and suits the cooler months. The Jimny G.O.A.T and RRC Casual tees use it.

For Mumbai, Chennai or Kolkata summers, 240 GSM is the comfortable choice. For Delhi, Chandigarh or Pune between November and February, the 260 GSM tees work well on their own in the day and under a hoodie at night.

Sizing by Body Type

  • Short (under 5 feet 7): go one size lower and keep the hem at the belt. A front tuck shortens the line further.
  • Tall and slim: your regular size gives the classic drape; the extra length suits you, and the dropped shoulder adds width to a narrow frame.
  • Broad chest or gym build: your regular size, in 240 GSM or heavier. Heavy fabric skims the chest instead of clinging to it.
  • Some weight at the middle: your regular size in heavy fabric, so it falls straight past the stomach. Avoid sizing down, which can make it pull.

Frequently Asked Questions

Should I size down for an oversized t-shirt?

For a neat oversized look, yes: MotorBae recommends ordering one size lower than your regular fit. For the full dropped shoulder drape, take your usual size. Size up only if you are tall and want it very baggy.

How long should an oversized tee be?

The hem should sit between your belt and the middle of your hip. On MotorBae's oversized tees, M is 28 to 29 inches long, about one to two inches longer than its regular fit tee.

What is a drop shoulder t-shirt?

A drop shoulder tee has its shoulder seam set a few inches down the upper arm instead of on the shoulder bone. It gives the relaxed, sloping line that defines an oversized fit.

Is 240 GSM good for an oversized tee?

Yes. 240 GSM is heavy enough to hold an oversized shape and drape cleanly, while staying comfortable in warmer Indian cities. 260 GSM is a little stiffer and warmer, which suits winter.
