A T-Shirt Is Not Riding Gear

This needs saying before anything else. A biker t shirt India riders wear on a Sunday is clothing, not protection. Cotton shreds in a slide within a metre or two. Whatever tee you buy, it goes under a riding jacket with CE-rated armour at the shoulders, elbows and back, with a proper helmet, gloves and boots.

The rule: the tee is for comfort and style; the jacket is for protection. Our complete riding gear guide covers what you need, and our jacket guide explains what to look for in a riding jacket.

What to Wear Under a Riding Jacket

On the bike, the best bike rider t shirt is the one you forget you are wearing. Three things decide that.

Fabric weight: go light in heat

GSM is the weight of the fabric. Under a riding jacket in Indian heat, a 180 GSM cotton regular fit tee is the sweet spot: light enough to breathe, thick enough not to go see-through with sweat. The heavier 240 to 260 GSM terry tees are lovely off the bike, but under a jacket in May they hold heat and stay damp. Save those for the meet or for winter mornings.

Fit: regular, not oversized

A riding jacket is cut close so the armour stays over your joints. An oversized tee with dropped shoulders bunches up underneath it and rides up at the back when you lean forward. Wear a regular fit tee under gear and change into the oversized one when you arrive. Our regular fit range lives in the Regular Fit T-Shirt collection.

Cotton or technical fabric?

Cotton is comfortable and fine for city commutes and short rides. On long summer tours, a technical base layer that wicks sweat will keep you drier. Many riders do both: a technical layer for the highway, a cotton tee in the tank bag for the evening.

Care: Keeping a Riding Tee Fresh

Riding tees get more sweat than most. Wash them inside out in cold water after every ride, which is the care MotorBae gives for its printed and embroidered tees, and dry them out of harsh direct sun so prints and colours last longer. Do not leave a sweaty tee rolled up in a tank bag overnight; that is where the smell that never washes out comes from.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which bike rider t shirt is best in India?

Under a riding jacket, a light 180 GSM cotton regular fit tee or a technical base layer. For the meet, a heavier oversized or embroidered tee. MotorBae is our top pick for riders who want both, with Royal Enfield apparel for brand loyalists and Rynox or Viaterra for technical layers.

Can I wear an oversized t-shirt under a riding jacket?

It is not ideal. Riding jackets are cut close to keep the armour in place, and an oversized tee bunches up underneath. Wear a regular fit tee on the bike and change into the oversized one when you arrive.

Is a cotton t-shirt good for long rides?

For city rides and short trips, yes. On long summer tours, cotton holds sweat, so a technical base layer that wicks moisture keeps you drier. Many riders wear a technical layer on the highway and carry cotton tees for the evening.

Does a t-shirt protect me if I fall off the bike?

No. A t-shirt is clothing, not protective gear. Always wear a riding jacket with CE-rated armour over it, along with a certified helmet, gloves and boots.
