Official F1 Merchandise vs Fan Designs: Which Should You Buy?

Both have a place in a fan's wardrobe, but they do different jobs.

Official F1 merchandise

Official f1 merchandise is made under licence from Formula 1 or a team. A replica team shirt carries the sponsor logos, the team crest and the exact team colours. Buy official if you want to wear what the pit crew wears, or if you collect. Expect to pay a premium, and with international stores, duties on top.

Fan-designed tribute pieces

Fan designs celebrate a car, a driver number or a moment without copying team kit. They tend to be cleaner, wearable away from race day, and priced like normal streetwear. MotorBae's SF-26 and Audi F1 tees fall here: the artwork tells a story about the car rather than reproducing the team's sponsor layout.

Rule of thumb: if a cheap tee reproduces a full team kit with every sponsor logo, it is almost certainly not official. Genuine replicas are never priced like basic tees.

How to Spot Fake F1 Merchandise Online

Most of the fake F1 merchandise India buyers run into is sold on marketplaces and social media shops. Five checks catch nearly all of it.

  • Price that is too good. Genuine replica team shirts are never sold at basic tee prices.
  • Missing tags. Official items carry the team or F1 licence on the swing tag and the inner label. No tag, no licence.
  • Blurry logos. Zoom into the photos. Sponsor logos on copies are often slightly wrong in shape or colour.
  • Seller history. On marketplaces, check that the seller is the brand's verified store, not a generic reseller with a new account.
  • Old season, new listing. A "2026" shirt with last year's sponsors is a giveaway.

If you want team-inspired wear without the licence premium, buy openly fan-designed pieces from a brand that says so, rather than a copy pretending to be official.
