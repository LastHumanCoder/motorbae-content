RANK 02: BEST FOR THE COLLECTOR

Scale Model Cars

A die-cast model of the exact car they own or dream about is a classic for a reason. Check the scale: 1:64 models are pocket-sized and cheap, 1:43 sits nicely on a desk, and 1:18 models are display pieces with opening doors. Licensed models of popular Indian cars are harder to find than European ones, so start looking before the festive rush.

RANK 03: BEST PRACTICAL GIFT

A Car Care Kit

Anyone who washes their own car will use a proper kit: two buckets, a pH-neutral shampoo, microfibre towels and a quick detailer. Pick a kit from a known car care brand rather than a generic combo pack, and leave out polishing compounds unless you know they machine polish. Our car accessories guide covers what actually gets used.

RANK 04: BEST FOR DAILY DRIVERS

A Dashcam

Useful in Indian traffic, and something most drivers keep meaning to buy. Look for good night recording and a parking mode if they park on the street. Only buy this if they are comfortable with a wired install or you can arrange one.

RANK 05: BEST EXPERIENCE GIFT

A Track Day or Driving Experience

The most memorable car gift, and the most expensive. Track days at circuits like the Buddh International Circuit and Madras Motor Race Track run on specific dates, so check the calendar before you promise one as a Diwali surprise.
