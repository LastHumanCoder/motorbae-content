Layering Rules That Keep You Warm and Not Bulky

  • Thin to thick, inside to outside. Base layer thinnest, hoodie or knit in the middle, jacket outside.
  • Mix fits on purpose. Oversized tee under an oversized hoodie works off the bike. Under a fitted or riding jacket, switch to a regular fit hoodie.
  • One statement at a time. If the tee or hoodie has a big print, keep the jacket and pants plain.
  • Watch the hems. A tee hem showing a little below a hoodie looks intentional; a long tee hanging well below looks sloppy.
  • Colours: build around black, charcoal, navy, olive and off-white, then let the car print be the colour.

Frequently Asked Questions

What should men wear in winter in India?

Layers: a heavyweight tee as the base, a 300 GSM or heavier hoodie or a merino sweater in the middle, and a jacket suited to your city outside. Finish with relaxed cargos, jeans or sweatpants.

Is a hoodie enough for winter in India?

In Mumbai, Chennai, Bengaluru and Pune, a heavyweight hoodie over a tee is enough most days. In North India and hill stations, add a thermal underneath and a puffer or riding jacket on top.

What are the must-have winter clothes for men?

Two heavyweight tees, a layering tee or thermal, a heavy hoodie, a smart sweater, one jacket, two bottoms and a cap or beanie. MotorBae covers the hoodie, tees and sweatpants.

How do I layer without looking bulky?

Go thin to thick from the inside out, use a regular fit hoodie under fitted jackets, and keep only one bold print in the outfit.

What should I wear for a winter motorcycle ride?

A base layer, a regular fit hoodie, a riding jacket with CE armour, riding pants, gloves and a balaclava under the helmet. A hoodie is a warm layer, never protection.
