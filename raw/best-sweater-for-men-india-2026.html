Sweater vs Hoodie: Which Should You Buy?

Choose a sweater if you need to look smart: offices, family functions, Diwali visits, dinners out. Choose a heavyweight hoodie if you ride, travel, or want something you can machine wash every week without worrying about shape.

  • On the bike: hoodie, under a riding jacket. Knit fabric lets wind straight through. See our riding and winter jacket guide.
  • At work: merino crew or V-neck.
  • Weekends: either. A hoodie with cargos or track pants for men is the comfort pick; a crew neck knit with jeans is the smarter one.

How to Judge a Sweater in Two Minutes

You do not need a fabric degree to spot a sweater for men that will last. In the store, or as soon as a delivery arrives, check four things:

  • The label. Look for the wool or merino content first. A sweater sold as "wool" that is mostly acrylic will feel nice for a month and then pill.
  • The stretch test. Gently pull a section of the body sideways and let go. A good knit springs back; a weak one stays stretched.
  • The seams and cuffs. Ribbed cuffs and hem should be firm, and the shoulder seams should be linked neatly rather than cut and overlocked loosely.
  • The fit. The shoulder seam should sit on your shoulder, the sleeves should reach your wrist, and the hem should cover your belt. A sweater that is too short rides up as soon as you reach for something.

The same logic applies to hoodies: check the GSM on the listing, look at the rib on the cuffs, and make sure the hood is lined or heavy enough to hold its shape. MotorBae lists the GSM on every hoodie page, which makes that comparison easy.

Care and Pilling

Pilling, those small balls of fibre, comes from friction and short fibres, which is why cheap acrylic pills fastest. To slow it down, wash knits inside out on a gentle cycle in cold water, never tumble dry wool, and dry sweaters flat so they do not stretch on a hanger. A fabric shaver removes pills in a couple of minutes. Store knits folded rather than hung, so the shoulders do not stretch over the season.

Hoodies are simpler: inside out, cold water, low heat, and turn inside out before ironing so the print is never touched.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which sweater brand is best for men in India?

For smart knitwear, Uniqlo's merino, Monte Carlo and Marks & Spencer are reliable choices, with H&M for budget pieces. For a casual winter layer with car culture artwork, MotorBae's heavyweight hoodies are our top pick.

Is a hoodie warmer than a sweater?

A 300 GSM cotton hoodie is warmer than most mid-weight cotton or acrylic sweaters and blocks wind better. A good wool or merino sweater is warmer for its thickness and looks smarter.

Does MotorBae sell sweaters?

No. MotorBae makes heavyweight hoodies, tees, sweatpants and accessories. Its 300 to 320 GSM hoodies work as a casual alternative to a sweater.

How do I stop my sweater from pilling?

Wash it inside out on a gentle cold cycle, avoid tumble drying, and dry it flat. Wool and merino pill less than acrylic. A fabric shaver removes existing pills.

What should I wear under a sweater?

A thin tee, a thermal or a collared shirt. Avoid heavy tees under heavy knits, which makes the arms bulky.
