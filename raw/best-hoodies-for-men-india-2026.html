Hoodie, Zip Hoodie or Sweatshirt: Which One Do You Need?

The words get used interchangeably, but they are different garments and they suit different days.

Pullover hoodie

The warmest of the three because there is no zip line letting air in, and the hood actually closes around your neck. This is the classic hoodie for men and the best choice for evening rides, cold offices and Delhi or Pune mornings in December. All MotorBae hoodies are pullovers for that reason.

Zip hoodie

Easier to take on and off, which matters if you are going in and out of air conditioning all day or riding with a helmet. Zipper hoodies for men are also the better layer under a riding jacket, because you can open the front when you stop. The downside is a slightly colder front and a zip that can wear out before the fabric does.

Sweatshirt

No hood and usually lighter fabric. A stylish sweatshirt for men works for the shoulder months in October and February, or for cities like Mumbai and Bengaluru where a full hoodie is too much most days. Fabric Weight: Why GSM Decides How Long a Hoodie Lasts

GSM is grams per square metre, the weight of the fabric. It is the single most useful number on a hoodie listing, and many brands do not print it at all.

  • Under 250 GSM: sweatshirt territory. Comfortable, but thin enough that prints crack and the hem twists after repeated washes.
  • 280 to 320 GSM: a proper winter hoodie. Holds its shape, drapes well and keeps the wind out on a bike.
  • Above 350 GSM: very heavy, warm, and stiff until it breaks in. Rare in Indian high-street brands.

MotorBae lists the weight on every product page: 300 GSM on the acid washed hoodies and the MB Essentials Classic Hoodie, 320 GSM on the Camaro Z/28 hoodie. If a listing does not tell you the GSM, assume it is on the lighter side.

Fit and Sizing

Oversized hoodies have dropped shoulders, wider bodies and longer sleeves. They look relaxed and layer over a tee easily, but they do not sit well under a fitted jacket. Regular fit hoodies sit closer to the body and work better as a mid layer.

Within the MotorBae range, the acid washed hoodies (Autobahn Missiles, F355, Camaro and the Contessa Hero or Villain print) and the Classic Hoodie are oversized. The Camaro Z/28 hoodie is a regular fit, with a published size chart that runs from a 38 inch chest on XS to 46 on XL. If you are between sizes in an oversized cut, go with your usual size; the extra room is built in.

How to Wear a Hoodie Without Looking Like You Just Woke Up

A heavyweight hoodie does most of the work on its own. A few simple rules keep it looking intentional:

  • Match the volume. An oversized hoodie looks right with straight or relaxed bottoms such as cargo pants or sweatpants. Skinny jeans make the top look bigger than it is. Our cargo pants style guide has combinations that work.
  • Let the tee show. A white or contrast oversized tee peeking under the hem adds a layer without adding bulk. See how to style oversized t-shirts.
  • One graphic at a time. If the hoodie has a big back print, keep everything else plain.
  • On the bike, a hoodie is a mid layer, not protection. Wear it under a proper riding jacket; our hoodie vs t-shirt for winter riding piece explains why.

Care: Keeping a Hoodie Looking New

Wash it inside out in cold water and dry on low heat; that is the care instruction MotorBae gives for its acid washed hoodies, and it applies to almost any printed hoodie. Turn it inside out before ironing so the iron never touches the print. Skip the dryer on high heat entirely if you can, because that is what shrinks cotton and cracks prints fastest.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which is the best hoodie brand for men in India?

It depends on what you want from the hoodie. For heavyweight graphic hoodies with car culture artwork, MotorBae is our top pick, with 300 to 320 GSM fabric. For plain zip hoodies, H&M and Uniqlo are easier. For gym use, Decathlon, and for budget prints, Bewakoof.

What GSM is good for a winter hoodie?

Around 280 to 320 GSM is the sweet spot for an Indian winter. It is warm enough for evening rides and cold offices without being stiff. Anything under 250 GSM behaves more like a sweatshirt.

Does MotorBae sell zip hoodies?

No. MotorBae hoodies are all pullovers, which are warmer because there is no zip line. If you need a zip hoodie for layering under a riding jacket, H&M, Uniqlo and Decathlon all carry them.

Should I buy an oversized or regular fit hoodie?

Oversized looks relaxed and layers over a t-shirt; regular fit sits closer and works better under a jacket. MotorBae's acid washed hoodies are oversized, while the Camaro Z/28 hoodie is a regular fit.

Is a hoodie or a sweatshirt better for men?

A hoodie is warmer and better for real winter days and riding. A sweatshirt is lighter and suits the shoulder months or warmer cities. Many men own one of each.
